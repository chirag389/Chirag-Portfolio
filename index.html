About Me

Hi, I’m Chirag Mahajan, a Product Designer with 3+ years of experience working on B2B SaaS, AI-powered products, and marketing tools.

I like working on products where the problem isn’t always clear at the beginning. I enjoy understanding the problem, figuring out how things should work, and turning complex workflows into simple and easy-to-use experiences.

I work across UX, UI, information architecture, interaction design, and design systems, and I usually stay involved from the early product thinking to the final design. I also work closely with product and engineering teams to make sure what I design actually works in the real product. I’ve worked across customer management, communication, automation, content, and marketing products. Different domains, same thing I enjoy: figuring out the problem and making the product make sense.
