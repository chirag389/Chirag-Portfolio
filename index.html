About Me

I like solving complicated product problems.

I'm a Product Designer who enjoys working on products where the problem isn't immediately obvious. I work across product thinking, UX, information architecture, interaction design, UI and design systems to turn ambiguous requirements and complex workflows into clear product experiences — across customer systems, communication, automation, content and marketing products. The domain can change. What interests me is understanding the problem, finding the right structure, and designing something that makes sense for the people using it.

I care less about following a design process and more about finding the right way to solve the problem.
